Profile / Brand & Packaging Leadership

From making forms. To building brands.

Over 11 years, my work has moved from product and exhibition design to packaging systems, team supervision and production management. The common thread is turning complex requirements into clear work that can be produced, scaled and sold.

Current role Brand & Packaging Development Supervisor

The connecting idea

The work changed. The principle stayed.

I began by learning form, proportion and how physical objects occupy space. Packaging taught me to connect visual decisions with materials, cost, manufacturing and shelf behaviour. Leadership added another layer: people need the same clarity as products do.

Clear direction. Practical systems. Work that reaches the market.

Career journey

How the role grew.

A progression from making individual objects to building the systems, teams and production relationships around them.

012009–2014

Foundation / Form

Learning to see objects in three dimensions.

A Sculpture degree with Second-Class Honors built a strong foundation in proportion, material and spatial thinking. The first professional role at Element 26 extended that thinking into furniture, environmental graphics, exhibitions and on-site installation.

  • BFA Sculpture, Khon Kaen University
  • Assistant Product Designer, Element 26
022014–2016

Discipline / Production

Learning what makes design producible.

As a Junior Graphic Designer at D.H.A. Siamwalla, the focus shifted to print-ready artwork, production accuracy and supplier coordination. This stage built the technical discipline that still guides every design review today.

  • Print-ready artwork and pre-press
  • Supplier coordination and production accuracy
032016–2018

Ownership / Mentoring

Moving from execution to design ownership.

The Senior Graphic Designer role expanded into packaging and campaign assets across mass and premium segments. Collaboration with marketing became more strategic, while mentoring junior designers and reviewing print proofs introduced the first formal leadership responsibilities.

  • Brand consistency across campaign and packaging
  • Junior designer mentoring and proof review
042018–Present

Leadership / Brand systems

Building the system around the work.

As Brand & Packaging Development Supervisor, the role connects brand strategy, packaging architecture, team supervision and end-to-end print production. This includes leading a team of 2–3 designers, creating project codes and workflow tracking, reviewing performance, managing 50+ SKUs and coordinating Flexo, Gravure and Offset suppliers.

  • KIOKU repositioning and launch strategy
  • Workflow, quality control and multi-SKU governance
052024–Present

Business / Strategy

Adding the business lens.

The Executive MBA at NIDA strengthens the ability to connect creative decisions with revenue, cost, portfolio priorities and stakeholder expectations. It gives the design process a wider frame: not only what looks right, but what creates value and can be sustained.

  • Executive MBA, NIDA
  • Creative vision connected to business strategy

End-to-end production management

Management approach

A clear operating rhythm for a small team.

The goal is not to monitor people more. It is to make priorities, workload, quality and support easier to see.

01

Translate the brief

Turn business and marketing objectives into a clear scope, priority and definition of done.

02

Assign with context

Match work to skill and capacity, using project codes, schedules and task logs to keep ownership visible.

03

Review to improve

Use quality, revisions and delivery patterns to give specific feedback and identify where support is needed.

04

Connect to production

Bring material, process, cost and vendor constraints into the design conversation before they become production problems.
